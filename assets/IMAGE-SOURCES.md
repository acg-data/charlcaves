### Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide cinematic website hero, landscape 16:9, high resolution. Reference image: the attached Charleston Caves bar is the interior style reference; extend its architectural world into a complete room, maintaining dark walnut paneling, black veined marble counter, cognac leather stools, aged brass foot rails, warm shaded lamps and softly backlit bottle shelves. Primary request: exactly FIVE clearly adult men, approximately ages 35–60, enjoying an evening together at this private home bar, smoking cigars and talking naturally. Show all five distinct men together, some seated at the bar and others leaning nearby, natural candid gestures, neat relaxed shirts and sports jackets, realistic hands, subtle wisps of cigar smoke catching warm light, a few lowball glasses. No other people. Sophisticated, welcoming social scene, not staged lineup. Wide architectural editorial photograph from across the room so we see the complete bar and surrounding lounge; people grouped in the central-to-right two thirds, left third quieter and darker for website heading overlay. Warm amber practical lighting balanced with shadow detail, dark brown, tobacco leather and brass palette; photographic realism and tactile materials. No lettering, no logos, no watermarks. These are fictional adults in an illustrative interior concept, not real clients or founders.
