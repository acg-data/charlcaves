A few things
you might wonder.

You don’t need every detail figured out to start a conversation.

What can we create together?

Private bars and lounges, golf rooms, home theaters, garages, game rooms, studies and retreats. Start with an existing room or an idea; the first conversation helps define the scope.

How much will my room cost?

There isn’t one price for a custom room. Size, existing conditions, finishes and equipment all shape the scope. Share what you have in mind and any budget you want the design to work toward.

How long does a project take?

The schedule depends on the room, design decisions, any required approvals and material or equipment lead times. Timing should be discussed once the scope and site conditions are understood.

Do I need plans or a finished idea?

No. A few photos, approximate room dimensions and a sense of how you want to use the space make a useful starting point. You can begin with just the idea.

Where do you work?

Charleston, South Carolina. Include your location in the inquiry so we can discuss your project and confirm service availability.

Are these finished client projects?

The rooms shown here are illustrative concepts, including AI-generated imagery. They communicate design possibilities; your room’s layout, materials and details would be developed around your brief.
