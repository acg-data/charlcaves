Work out the room.
Then build it.

The layout comes first. Finishes and equipment should fit the room, the budget and the way you’ll use it.

  1. Talk through the space

    Tell us where it is, how you want to use it and what you have in mind for the budget. Room photos and rough dimensions help.

  2. Set the layout and scope

    Work through the drawings, equipment, lighting and finishes. Agree the scope and schedule before construction starts.

  3. Build to the agreed plan

    Bring the room together, from the built-in pieces to the final fittings.

Before you start.

What does a custom room cost?

Size, existing conditions, equipment and finish choices set the cost. Include a budget range in your inquiry so the scope can be discussed against it.

How long will it take?

That depends on the scope, any required approvals and equipment or material lead times. A schedule is agreed once the project is defined.

What should I send first?

Your location, the type of room and a short description are enough to start. Approximate dimensions and any equipment you already own are useful too.
